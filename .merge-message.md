merge main into the crate wave

`main` moved 753 files while the wave was being built: the ci/shepherd
stabilization, the SIS import lifecycle (PR #262), the curriculum parity
(#265), the admin routes (#263, #266) and the account reports (#263). The
wave and those PRs touched the same twenty crates, so this is the union of
two implementations of the same routes.

How it was resolved
- Where the two sides added different things, both are kept: the route
  registrations, the handlers, the schema columns and the migrations.
- Where they rewrote the same handler, main's version wins: it has been
  through CI and the recorded corpus, the wave's has not. That is
  `star-courses` (the CourseJson shape), `star-lti` (the registration
  lifecycle, which main moved out of `registrations.rs` and into
  `lib.rs`) and the GraphQL root (main's inline `Query` gave way to the
  wave's `query_root.rs`, with main's `media_object` field merged into it).
- Where both sides registered the same route, the second registration goes:
  `POST /files/update_word_count` and `POST /rce_linked_file_instfs_ids`
  were in both `star-files/src/lib.rs` and `star-files/src/file_gaps.rs`,
  and the wave's `/courses/{courseId}/assignments` was registered twice
  with POST. The `router_conflicts` gate finds every one of these; it is
  what turned the duplicate registrations into a failure instead of a
  panic at boot.
- The wave's migrations move onto the numbers `main` freed: 0264-0272,
  not 0240-0248. The numbers the workers had self-assigned (0262-0264,
  0344-0346, three of them the same) are gone, and no migration number is
  reused.
- The GraphQL root keeps both sides' fields: the wave's thirty root
  fields plus main's `media_object`.

The tree is clean: `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets` is warning-free,
`cargo fmt --check` passes and the router conflict gate is green.
